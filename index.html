Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-04-30

What Furnish is

Furnish is a mobile and web app that uses AI to redesign your rooms. You upload a photo, share your style preferences, and we generate a redesigned version with shoppable furniture suggestions.

What we collect

  • Room photos you upload
  • Style preferences and quiz answers
  • Free-form text you type in "Tell Us More" entries (profile-scoped) and pre-generation room context (per-room)
  • Account info — email, name, and profile photo (from Google Sign-In; for guest accounts a temporary device-bound identifier)
  • Device identifiers and IP address
  • Usage data — which screens you view, which items you tap, which redesigns you generate
  • Affiliate click events — when you tap "Shop" on an item

Why we collect it

  • Photos — so the AI can generate a redesigned version of your room
  • Preferences and free-form text — so redesigns match your actual taste
  • Account info — so you can sign in, sync across devices, and recover your data
  • Device data — for security, fraud prevention, and analytics
  • Usage data — to understand which features work and where to improve
  • Click events — so retailers can attribute affiliate purchases to Furnish (this is how we stay free)

Who sees the data

Furnish uses several third-party services. Each receives only the specific data needed to perform its job.

  • Supabase — cloud database, authentication, and storage. Holds your account, profiles, redesigns, and uploaded photos.
  • Google Sign-In — handles authentication when you sign in with Google. Receives only basic sign-in data (email, name).
  • Google AI (Gemini) — processes your room photos and generates redesigned images. Photos are transmitted to Google AI during generation; Google's data-handling terms apply.
  • OpenAI (GPT-5.4) — tags and analyzes generated images to identify furniture and decor. Generated images are transmitted to OpenAI; raw uploaded photos are not.
  • Skimlinks — handles affiliate click attribution. When you tap "Shop", an opaque-hash click event is sent to Skimlinks; no personal information is shared.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — handles app distribution and any future in-app purchases. Their respective privacy policies apply.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your photos with anyone outside the AI providers above.

How long we keep data

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your photos, redesigns, profiles, and preferences are removed within 30 days. Aggregated and anonymized analytics may be retained longer to improve the service.

  • Access your data — email support@furnish.live and we'll send a copy of what we hold for your account. In-app export is on the way.

FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions. If yours isn't here, email support@furnish.live.

How do I make my redesigns match my style better?

Three ways. First, update your preferences anytime — tap the Preferences tab in the bottom nav and adjust your answers. Second, add a "Tell Us More" entry — anything you'd want a designer to know about you (warm tones, no orange, two cats). These accumulate as bubbles on your Preferences tab. Third, before generating, use the optional pre-generation textbox to share specifics about this room — what mood you want, what you're trying to achieve.

Are these the actual prices for items?

We display retailer prices at the time of generation, but prices change. When you tap "Shop" on an item, you'll see the current price on the retailer's site. Always check there before purchasing.

What happens to my room photos?

Your photos are uploaded to secure cloud storage and processed by AI to generate your redesign. They're stored on your account so you can revisit redesigns anytime. We don't sell or share your photos. To delete a specific photo or your entire account, contact support@furnish.live. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Why are some items "Coming Soon"?

We're constantly building Furnish. Features marked "Coming Soon" are on our roadmap and will roll out as Pro features. You'll see them in your app before they launch — and you'll be the first to use them.

How does Furnish make money if it's free?

When you click "Shop" on an item we suggest and you make a purchase from the retailer, Furnish earns a small commission. You pay the same price you would otherwise. This is how Furnish stays free for you. See "Affiliate Disclosure" in Support for details.

Why don't I see exact items I can buy from each design?

Our AI suggests items that match the look in your redesign, then we connect you to retailers that sell similar pieces. If you don't see a "Shop" link on something, that item may not be available through our partner retailers — but the visual concept is yours to use as inspiration.

What's the difference between Saved Homes, Saved Rooms, and Saved Items?

Saved Homes is your collection of designed rooms grouped together as one home — like a digital "look book" for your living space. Saved Rooms are individual room redesigns you've kept aside. Saved Items are specific products you've heart-saved from any redesign. All three are accessible from the Saved tab.

Can I redo a redesign with different choices?

Yes. Your photo and preferences stay saved. Tap the redesign in your Saved Rooms or Recent Designs, choose to redesign again, and adjust your preferences or pre-generation context to get a different result.

How do I delete my account?

For now, email support@furnish.live with a request to delete your account. We'll permanently remove your photos, preferences, and account info within 30 days. We're working on adding in-app account deletion soon.

My redesign doesn't fit my actual room. What can I do?

AI is still learning. The closer your photo matches good lighting and a full-room view, the better the redesign. Try retaking the photo with bright daylight, the entire room visible in frame, and minimal shadows. Then regenerate. If it still feels off, tell us in the post-generation survey or email support@furnish.live.
