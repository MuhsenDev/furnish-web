How Furnish Makes Money

Plain-English version, then the legal version.

The short version

We earn a small commission when you buy through Furnish — and that's how we keep AI redesigns free. No extra cost to you; the price is identical to going to the retailer directly.

Furnish Pro ($5.99/month) is the paid plan that funds the rest of what we do. Unlimited redesigns and the full catalog stay free for everyone.

What we don't do

  • We don't mark up prices.
  • We don't share your purchase data with the retailer beyond a click identifier.
  • We don't recommend an item just because it pays more — recommendations come from your style profile.

The legal bit

Furnish has affiliate / referral relationships with retailers including IKEA, Amazon, Wayfair, West Elm, Etsy, and Rugs USA. When you click a link from Furnish to one of these retailers and complete a purchase, Furnish may receive a commission. This material connection is disclosed in compliance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's 16 CFR Part 255 ("Endorsement Guides"). Per FTC guidance, these connections are disclosed because they may affect the weight or credibility of our recommendations.
