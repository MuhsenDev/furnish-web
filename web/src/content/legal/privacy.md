This document is being finalized through a legal compliance service
(Termly or iubenda) and will be reviewed by counsel before launch.

The full Privacy Policy will cover the following sections required
by Document 8 §4:

1. What data Furnish collects
2. How that data is used
3. Data retention periods (photos auto-delete 30 days post-redesign)
4. Third-party data sharing (retailers, Plausible, Skimlinks, Supabase)
5. User rights (access, deletion, portability)
6. Cookies and tracking (Plausible sets none; Skimlinks sets a tracking cookie on /blog/* once enabled)
7. Children's privacy (COPPA)
8. International transfers
9. Policy update notification process
10. Contact information

In the meantime, the high-level data flow is:

- The photos you upload are processed for your redesign and
  auto-deleted from our servers 30 days later.
- Account data (email, sign-in provider) lives in our Supabase
  database, deletable from Settings or by emailing
  hello@furnish.live.
- Plausible analytics is privacy-respecting and sets no cookies.
- Skimlinks affiliate tracking is active only on `/blog/*`
  pages once the Skimlinks SDK is enabled. It sets a tracking
  cookie that you can opt out of at the Skimlinks site.
- We are committed to GDPR and CCPA compliance.

Email **hello@furnish.live** with any questions about how Furnish
handles your data.
